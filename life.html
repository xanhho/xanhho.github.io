My Inspirations

I'm thankful to the Shen Yun dancers. They are around the same age as I am, but their hard work inspires me greatly. I am especially grateful to Melody Qin and Henry Hung. Their videos have motivated me whenever I lose focus, feel lazy, or get bored. They also remind me of the meaning behind the phrase, "Hard work always pays off."
