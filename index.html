About Me

Hello, I am Xanh Ho.

I am currently a researcher at NII LLMC, Japan. I received my Bachelor's degree from the VNUHCM - University of Science, and my Ph.D. from The Graduate University for Advanced Studies (SOKENDAI) in affiliation with the National Institute of Informatics, Japan.

I had the privilege of being supervised by Prof. Akiko Aizawa for my Ph.D. and by Dr. Nhung Nguyen for my Bachelor's degree. During my Ph.D. and postdoctoral research, I have also had the opportunity to work with Saku Sugawara, Florian Boudin, and Atsuhiro Takasu.
